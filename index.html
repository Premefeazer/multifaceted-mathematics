How a lesson works.

  • One idea per screen

    Each screen asks a single question, so nothing gets buried in a wall of text.

  • You try first, we explain after

    Make a guess before you see the answer. A wrong guess still teaches you something.

  • Hints, not answers

    Stuck? A hint points you in a direction. It never hands you the answer.

  • Five minutes, tops

    A lesson is short enough to fit between two classes.
