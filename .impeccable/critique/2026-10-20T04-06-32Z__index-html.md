## Priority issues
- [P1] Page promises "guess first" but nothing is playable. Lesson card is static and sits ~1000px down on mobile. Fix: real option buttons with per-option explanations, move card under the ticker. Command: delight.
- [P1] Hero solid hides meaning and affordance. Put topic symbols on the 8 topic faces, add a one-time drag cue, tapped topic face highlights its ticker item, outline instead of white for the selected face. Command: onboard.
- [P2] Motion and keyboard gaps. Ticker not pausable by touch or keyboard; canvas can't select a face by keyboard; readout stale and may shift layout. Command: harden.
- [P2] Generic scaffolding dilutes the idea. Cut roadmap to one line, return the solid into the closing panel. Command: layout.
- [P3] CTA labeling and teacher trust. One primary action above the fold, consistent destination name, surface topic questions, add About line in footer. Command: clarify.
